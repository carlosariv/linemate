Standard operating procedure for handling raw meat.
Wash hands before and after handling raw meat. Use designated cutting boards and utensils.
Keep raw meat refrigerated until preparation and prevent contact with ready-to-eat foods.
Clean and sanitize all food-contact surfaces after preparation.
