A simple pasta recipe using tomatoes, basil, garlic, and olive oil.
Ingredients include pasta, canned tomatoes, fresh basil, garlic, olive oil, salt, and pepper.
Cook the pasta until al dente, prepare the tomato sauce separately, then combine and serve.
